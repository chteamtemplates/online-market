Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?
Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days within the US. International orders typically arrive in 7–14 business days. Express options are available at checkout. You'll receive a tracking link via email once your order ships.
What is your return policy?
We accept returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be in original condition with tags attached. Returns are completely free — we'll cover the prepaid shipping label. Refunds are processed within 5 business days of receipt.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Cash on Delivery in select regions. All payments are secured with 256-bit SSL encryption.
Are products authentic and guaranteed?
Yes — every Trendora product is 100% authentic and crafted from premium materials. Each item ships with a certificate of authenticity and a 1-year quality warranty.
Do you offer international shipping?
Yes — we ship worldwide. Shipping fees and delivery times depend on the destination and are calculated at checkout. Customs and import duties (if any) are the responsibility of the recipient.
How do I find the right size?
Each product page includes a detailed size guide with measurements. If you're between sizes, we recommend going up. Still unsure? Contact our styling team and we'll help you choose.

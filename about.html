Our Story

Where heritage tailoring meets contemporary design.

Founded 2018

Crafted with intention.
Worn with confidence.

Trendora was founded on a simple belief: real elegance is timeless. We started in a small atelier in New York with a single goal — to create pieces that age beautifully and earn their place in your wardrobe season after season.

Today, our designs are crafted across heritage workshops in Italy, Portugal, and Turkey. Each garment is hand-finished, each accessory thoughtfully sourced, and each detail considered with care.

We don't follow trends. We build collections that stand alone — refined, considered, and made to last.
